5. “GRAVITY-REDUCTION” TERMINOLOGY

The terms “gravity-reduction,” “gravity assist,” “simulated gravity relief,” “low-G,” “load relief,” 
and similar terminology are used as conceptual descriptions of the intended user experience and engineering objective.

They should not automatically be interpreted as claims that the footwear literally cancels Earth's gravitational field.

The current repository documentation itself explicitly distinguishes simulated effects from actual gravity cancellation and states:

True gravity cancellation is not yet achievable.

Therefore, future technical documents should preferably distinguish among:

A. Actual gravitational modification

A physical alteration of the gravitational field.

B. Effective load reduction

Reduction in forces transmitted through the foot or body through mechanical, pneumatic, electromagnetic, or structural assistance.

C. Perceived weight/inertia reduction

A change in the user's perception or experience of loading, acceleration, impact, or movement.

D. Energy-return assistance

Recovery and return of mechanical energy during the gait cycle.

The project may explore B, C, and D without claiming that A has been achieved.
