Wiz-Dimensional Road 5K V-4

Elite Lightweight Road Racing Shoe (Concept Specification)



The Wiz-Dimensional Road 5K V-4 is a conceptual evolution focused exclusively on road racing from one mile through 10 kilometers, 
with peak optimization for the 5K. The design philosophy emphasizes minimizing mass while integrating a carbon spring propulsion system,
Pebax-based cushioning, silicone micro-gel damping, and a lightweight structural upper into a cohesive racing platform.
This version is intended as a dedicated road racing concept rather than an all-purpose performance shoe. 
The original Lite V-3 concept established the carbon spring, Pebax/EVA midsole, silicone gel pod system,
honeycomb outsole, and carbon-Kevlar upper that this design builds upon.

Engineering Summary



The Wiz-Dimensional Road 5K V-4 combines a low-top carbon-Kevlar upper, a tapered CFRP propulsion plate,
Pebax-dominant superfoam, a multi-chamber silicone micro-gel damping system, and a lightweight honeycomb road 
outsole into a single conceptual racing platform. The design aims to reduce rotational mass, increase cadence, 
improve propulsion efficiency, and provide responsive cushioning while maintaining stability through structural
integration of the spring plate, foam, and outsole. As with the earlier concept, these specifications represent 
a design proposal and engineering hypothesis rather than the measured performance of a commercially tested shoe.
